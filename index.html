Common Questions

Quick answers when you need them most.

How fast can you get here?

We answer 24/7 and target an on-site response of 90 minutes or less for emergencies in our service area. The sooner we start, the less the damage spreads.

Do you work with my insurance?

Yes — we work with all home insurance carriers. We document the damage thoroughly and provide the reports your adjuster needs.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the San Francisco Bay Area and California's Central Valley, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Are you licensed and certified?

Yes. Rextoration is licensed, insured, and IICRC certified — the industry standard for professional restoration work.

What should I do while I wait for you to arrive?

Stay safe first — don't enter areas with structural, electrical, or standing-water hazards. If it's safe, shut off the water source or power to affected areas, and take photos for your records. We'll handle the rest when we arrive.
