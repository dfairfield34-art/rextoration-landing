This Country Was Built in 1850. We Keep It Standing.

The Mother Lode isn't tract housing — it's living history. Brick storefronts from the Gold Rush, miners'-era Victorians, hand-built cabins under the pines. Old buildings mean old plumbing, old wiring, and old wood — and when water, fire, or mold gets into them, they deserve better than a tear-it-all-out crew. That's the work we love most.

Downtown & Main Street

Historic Storefronts & Brick Buildings

Sonora's Washington Street, Columbia, Jamestown — century-old brick and timber with aging pipes above and stone cellars below. When a line bursts over a shop or mold blooms in a basement, we do careful, surgical demolition, dry the structure without wrecking original material, and document everything for the insurance claim.

Victorian & Mining-Era

The Old Homes

Plaster walls, true-dimension lumber, millwork you can't buy anymore. We treat older homes like they matter — drying and restoring original wood and plaster where it can be saved, cutting only what can't, and rebuilding so the repair belongs to the house.

Under the Pines

Cabins & Mountain Homes

Twain Harte, Mi-Wuk, Arnold — cabins that sit empty for weeks while a pipe freezes or a roof leaks. We respond fast, handle it while you're away, work with your insurance, and get the place ready for the next season.

Common Questions

Quick answers when you need them most.

What areas of the foothills do you serve?

We serve Sonora and the surrounding Gold Country foothills 24/7 — Jamestown, Soulsbyville, Twain Harte, Columbia, Tuolumne, Mi-Wuk Village and Groveland in Tuolumne County, plus Angels Camp, Murphys and Arnold in Calaveras County, and down to Oakdale.

Do you handle wildfire and smoke damage?

Yes. Wildfire smoke and soot are a fact of life up here. We do professional smoke and soot removal, odor neutralization, and full fire-damage restoration — and we document everything for your insurance claim.

Can you help with a burst or frozen pipe at elevation?

Absolutely. Cold snaps in the higher towns like Twain Harte and Arnold burst pipes every winter. We respond 24/7, stop the water, dry the structure, and repair the damage.

My cabin or second home flooded while I was away — can you help?

Yes — undetected leaks in vacation homes and cabins are one of the most common foothill jobs we handle. Call us and we can coordinate access, mitigate the damage, and keep you updated remotely throughout.

Can you work on older and historic buildings?

Yes — it's some of our favorite work. Gold Rush-era brick, Victorian plaster, true-dimension lumber: old structures need careful, surgical demolition and drying that protects the original material. We restore what can be saved, rebuild what can't, and document everything for your insurance.

Do you work with my insurance?

Yes — we work with all home insurance carriers. We document the damage thoroughly and provide the reports your adjuster needs.

What should I do while I wait for you to arrive?

Stay safe first — don't enter areas with structural, electrical, or standing-water hazards. If it's safe, shut off the water source or power to affected areas, and take photos for your records. We'll handle the rest when we arrive.
